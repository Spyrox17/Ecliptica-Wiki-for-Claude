Enemies refers to the many antagonistic creatures and entities you will find through the game.

Enemies are phase-based and can only spawn from a certain point onwards and can stop spawning at different phases too.

Like bosses, enemies also inflict <font color="LIGHTGREEN">buffs</font> or <font color="RED">debuffs</font> and attack with different <font color="GREEN">ELEMENTAL</font> types. Enemies also have different elemental affinities and will be added at some point in the future.
