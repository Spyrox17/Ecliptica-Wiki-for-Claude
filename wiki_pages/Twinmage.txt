= Twinmage =
As much as you've always wanted to become a powerful sorcerer, some people just do not have the necessary talent and innate ability. But, you could always just rely on a pair of Elemental Hands to make up for that.

== Skills ==
[[File:Twinmagehands.png|The varying elemental hands of Twinmage|alt=The varying elemental hands of Twinmage|thumb|484x484px]]
Twinmage is unique in that rather than a specific primary or secondary, it can instead choose between six elements for each hand.

Firing only one hand will increase your attack speed by 75%

* This is a multiplicative increase to your BASE attack speed.
* This means it does not reduce your status effect chance.

=== [[File:Twinmage-Fireball.png|frameless|64x64px]] Hand: Flaming Hand ===
Launch a fireball, dealing 30 <font COLOR="ORANGE">FIRE</font> damage. 

10% chance to inflict [[File:Status Fire.png|frameless|20x20px]]<font COLOR="ORANGE">BURNING</font> status effect.

Has a projectile speed of 16m/s and a max distance of 50m.

=== [[File:Twinmage-Ice-Missile.png|frameless|64x64px]] Hand: Ice Hand ===
Launch a frost missile, dealing 30 <font COLOR="LIGHTBLUE">FROST</font> damage.

10% chance to inflict [[File:Status Frozen.png|frameless|20x20px]]<font COLOR="LightBlue">FREEZING</font> status effect. 

Has a projectile speed of 20m/s and a max distance of 50m.

=== [[File:Twinmage-Thunderbolt.png|frameless|64x64px]] Hand: Electric Hand ===
Cast a bolt of lightning, dealing 30 <font COLOR="YELLOW">ELECTRIC</font> damage. 

10% chance to inflict [[File:Status Stun.png|frameless|20x20px]]<font COLOR="YELLOW">PARALYZED</font> status effect.

Hit scan with a max distance of 50m.

=== [[File:Twinmage-Windblade.png|frameless|64x64px]] Hand: Wind Blade ===
Launch a whirlwind, dealing 30 <font COLOR="GREY">PHYSICAL</font> damage.

10% chance to inflict <font COLOR="RED">[[File:Status Bleed.png|frameless|20x20px]]BLEEDING</font> status effect.

Has a projectile speed of 18m/s and a max distance of 50m.

=== [[File:Twinmage-Light-Ball.png|frameless|64x64px]] Hand: Divine Hand ===
Launch a ball of light, dealing 35 <font COLOR="#e4e08c">LUMINOUS</font> damage. 

10% chance to apply [[File:Status Weak.png|frameless|20x20px]]<font COLOR="BROWN">WEAKENED</font> status effect.

Has a projectile speed of 24m/s and a max distance of 50m.

=== [[File:Twinmage-Dark-Ball.png|frameless|64x64px]] Hand: Profane Hand ===
Launch a shadow missile, dealing 20 <font COLOR="PURPLE">SHADOW</font> damage. 

10% chance to apply [[File:Status Breach.png|frameless|20x20px]]<font COLOR="#7f7f7f">BREACHED</font> status effect. 

Has a projectile speed of 12m/s and a max distance of 50m.

=== [[File:Twinmage-Teleport.png|frameless|64x64px]] Utility: Quickwarp ===
Teleport 15 meters to where you are pointing. 

6 second cooldown.
