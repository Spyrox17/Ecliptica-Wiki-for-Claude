Classes are your first choice in playing Ecliptica and there are currently 8 of them to choose from:

Each boast unique combat abilities, HP values and evasive moves to help with your class.

Click on the Class Icon to learn more about each class.

You may change your Class mid-run at the Eclipse Black Hole when in The In-Between, however you will only get 95% of your total tokens at that point back.
