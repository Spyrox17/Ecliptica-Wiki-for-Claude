= Spellsword =
You may call yourself a Spellsword, but you were ever really strong enough to wield a sword skillfully. However, who said your magical abilities can't make up for that?

== Skills ==

=== [[File:Spellsword-Primary.png|frameless|64x64px]] Primary: Telekinetic Strike ===
Swing your sword, dealing 65 <font color="GREY">PHYSICAL</font> damage.  

10% chance to inflict <font COLOR="RED">[[File:Status Bleed.png|frameless|20x20px]]BLEEDING</font> status effect. 

=== [[File:Spellsword-Secondary.png|frameless|64x64px]] Secondary: Piercing Strike ===
Charge for up to X seconds and then release a mid-range piercing attack, dealing 85-170 <font color="GREY">PHYSICAL</font> damage.  

Up to 50% chance to inflict <font COLOR="RED">[[File:Status Bleed.png|frameless|20x20px]]BLEEDING</font> status effect. 

* Range, Damage, and Bleed chance increases with charge time.
* Charge time is reduced by Attack Speed.

=== [[File:Spellsword-Dash.png|frameless|64x64px]] Utility: Quick Dash ===
Perform a dash in your current movement direction.

* Has a very short period of I-frames at the beginning of the dash. 
* Can dash through purple attacks.

3 second cooldown.
