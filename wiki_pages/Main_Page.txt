=== Welcome to The Official Ecliptica Wiki! ===
Your one stop for all information regarding the VRChat Game Ecliptica.<span style="font-size: 20px;">'''<center>⚠️ It is suggested to view this Wiki in Dark Mode!
⛔ This game is in Patreon Playtest! You must have a Patreon Member to play. Any tier is acceptable, however Patreon Members must start the instance to grant access to everyone!
</center>'''</span>

This game involves several layers of levels, each one ending in a Boss Battle, with waves of enemies in-between. With an upgrade system, many selectable classes and whelms of enemies and bosses to fight, you won't get bored!

If you would like to support the development of Ecliptica, consider heading over to Mantibro's [https://www.patreon.com/mantibro Patreon Page]!

This game features a lot of contributor made enemies, bosses, effects, etc. Each will be listed in the below page, go check it out!
