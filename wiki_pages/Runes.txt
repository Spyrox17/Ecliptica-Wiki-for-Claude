Runes are powerful upgrades that you can equip before you begin your run, however, require you to complete a full run prior to unlock one at random.

You can equip runes in the beginning lobby at the Workshop Anvil.
[[File:Workshop.png|center|frameless|600x600px]]

There are four slots you can equip runes to, though you will not see your first rune upgrade activate until you get to the [[Difficulty Modifier|Penumbral]] phase. It increases in phase requirement for each slot until [[Difficulty Modifier|Eclipse]].[[File:RuneHUD.png|center|frameless|580x580px]]

<center>'''⚠️ As of the Patreon Playtest Demo, you can only use the Penumbral and Antumbral Rune slots!'''</center>


Be careful! While powerful, runes will also activate a random, potent Runic Curse.

There are 17 runes currently as of Patreon Release.

== Curses ==
There are 14 Runic Curses that you can receive when your rune activates, each one received is random, and you can get the same one twice!

You will get one stack of a curse for your Penumbral Rune, two stacks of a curse for your Antumbral Rune and so on.
