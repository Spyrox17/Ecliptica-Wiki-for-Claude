Artifacts are a key form of progression in Ecliptica, they serve as an extra task to do before you can fight the [[Bosses|boss]] on each floor. 

Artifacts held actually dictates what [[Maps|Map]] you go to next. 

In future versions, Artifacts sacrificed will also dictate what boss you will face at the [[Difficulty Modifier|Eye of the Eclipse]], though as of the Patreon Demo Playtest, you will always face [[Bosses|Jim C. Bringer]]. 

There are currently seven artifacts, and each can give stat bonuses to everyone for the troubles of bringing them to the dimensional teleporter.

If an Artifact is the last objective before gathering to the Dimensional Teleporter, everyone will be granted a [[Status Effects|Haste]] status effect until one is successfully sacrificed to the Dimensional Teleporter.
