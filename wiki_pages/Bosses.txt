Bosses are incredibly strong [[enemies]] which serve as your final obstacle for completing a floor, and are the primary threat of Ecliptica. 

* Certain bosses may only appear if your [[Difficulty Modifier|phase]] meets a certain threshold.
* Some bosses are split between phases, this is shown in the "Phase Requirement" column.
** Early meaning "First Half" and Late meaning "Second Half". 
** Any boss with just one phase in its requirement can appear at any time during that phase.

Bosses all boast unique abilities, move-sets and also have their own Weaknesses and Resistances.

* [[Status Effects]] triggered against bosses are less effective, though will still work.
* <font COLOR="PURPLE">PURPLE</font> attacks will bypass your Defense, so if you see it charge one up, run away!
* Being the Last Man Standing against a Boss in an instance with 5 or more people will grant you the [[File:Status adrenaline.png|frameless|16x16px]]<font COLOR="#FFFF33">ADRENALINE </font>[[Status Effects|status effect]].
Some bosses are contributor made, or voiced by other contributors, see the full list [[Contributors|here]].

== Eye of the Eclipse Bosses ==
Eye of the Eclipse bosses are unique, such that they can only be fought if the correct [[Artifacts|Artifact]] was brought to the Dimensional Gate AND if it was the most sacrificed one. As of the v0.5 Patreon Playtest, only Jim C. Bringer exists as the final boss, though more will be added and will require their associated artifact to be sacrificed the most to face them.
