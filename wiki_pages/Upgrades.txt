== Upgrades ==
Upgrades are powerful boosts you will spend your tokens in The In-Between. Upgrades range from minor stat increases to completely changing the way you play the game. 

Upgrades are split between three NPCs, each with their own themes. 

Note that despite many stat-related upgrade's stack types being marked as linear, a majority of stats themselves may be inherently hyperbolic or be subject to falloff. See the [[Stats]] page for more details on individual stats. 

All upgrades are additive increases unless stated otherwise. 

Purchasing an upgrade requires [[Tokens]]. 

If you are unsure on what "Stack Types" mean, please visit this [[Stack Types|page]] for more information. 

If you are playing solo, Upgrades such as Clutch or Kick and Sacrificial Lamb will be blacklisted from the shop.
