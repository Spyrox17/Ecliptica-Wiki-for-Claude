Status Effects include various buffs, debuffs, or other miscellaneous effects which may be applied to the player or enemies. There are 24 Status Effects in Ecliptica.

Status effects apply multiple stacks of the effect which have varying durations rather than being applied for specific amounts of time, which will be detailed here. 

== Buffs ==
Buffs are effects that are applied through normal gameplay and benefit you in some way.
