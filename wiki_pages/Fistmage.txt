= Fistmage =
Someone as musclebrained as you has no reason to become a sorcerer, but you persevered anyway and made it work somehow.

Powerful hybrid tank/damage class with a strong focus on counterattacks.

== Skills ==

=== [[File:Fistmage-Aura-Fist.png|frameless|64x64px]] Primary: Aura Fist ===
Charge for up to 1 second and release to punch, dealing 55-100 <font COLOR="GREY">PHYSICAL</font> damage based on charge level. 

=== [[File:Fistmage-Deflection.png|frameless|64x64px]] Secondary: Deflecting Swipe ===
Swipe, dealing 40 <font COLOR="GREY">PHYSICAL</font> damage.

After the swipe, gain a short window where the next instance of damage you take will be fully deflected. Successfully deflecting an attack will grant you 7 stacks of <font COLOR="#ff0000">[[File:Status Empower.png|frameless|20x20px]]EMPOWERED</font> and [[File:Status Defense.png|frameless|20x20px]]<font color="#12ebff">ENFORCED,</font> HEAL you for 25 health, and instantly reset the cooldown.

5 second cooldown.

=== [[File:Fistmage-Utility.png|frameless|64x64px]] Utility: Super Endurance ===
For 5 seconds, cleanse all negative status effects and enter an enduring stance. During this time you lose 75% movement speed, reduce damage taken by X%, gain 400% increased knockback resistance, and gain increased [[Aggro]] draw.

* All above stats are multipliers to your base stats and go above any caps.

12 second cooldown.
