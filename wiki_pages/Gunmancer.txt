= Gunmancer =
With enough cybernetic enhancements and a brain-interfaced Blast Cannon, anyone can become a Sorcerer these days. 

== Skills ==

=== Primary: ===

=== [[File:Gunmancer-Primary.png|frameless|64x64px]] VA-11 Blast Cannon ===
Fire a long-range hit scan attack every 2 seconds which deals 50 <font color="YELLOW">ELECTRIC</font> Damage. 

Additionally, apply a small explosion where your attack hits, dealing 50% of the projectile's Damage.

Has an 15% chance to apply the [[File:Status Stun.png|frameless|16x16px]]<font color="YELLOW">PARALYZED</font> debuff to enemies.

'''OR'''

=== [[File:Gunmancer-Flechette.png|frameless|64x64px]] Kinetic Fletchette ===
Fire a long-range projectile every X seconds which deals 75 <font COLOR="GREY">PHYSICAL</font> Damage. The projectile can pierce multiple enemies.

15% Chance to inflict the [[File:Status Bleed.png|frameless|20x20px]]<font COLOR="RED">BLEEDING</font> status effect.

=== Secondary: ===

=== [[File:Gunmancer-Firebomb.png|frameless|64x64px]] Firebomb ===
Launch an explosive projectile with high gravity every X seconds which deals 50 <font color="ORANGE">FIRE</font> Damage.

This attack explodes in a wide radius when it hits, dealing 100% of the projectile's Damage. 

=== [[File:Gumancer-Photon-Cannon.png|frameless|64x64px]] Photon Condenser ===
Fire fast bullets every 0.25 seconds which deals 10 <font COLOR="#e4e08c">LUMINOUS</font> Damage.

5% Chance to inflict the [[File:Status Weak.png|frameless|20x20px]]<font COLOR="#802525">WEAKENED</font> status effect. 

=== [[File:Gunmancer-Dark-Rifle.png|frameless|64x64px]] Antimatter Accelerator ===
Fire a scattered blast of five projectiles every X seconds which each deal 8 <font COLOR="PURPLE">SHADOW</font> Damage. Hold to narrow the spread.

5% Chance to inflict the [[File:Status Breach.png|frameless|20x20px]]<font COLOR="GREY">BREACHED</font> status effect.

=== [[File:Gumancer-Airblast.png|frameless|64x64px]] Utility: Airblast ===
While standing still, launch yourself upwards then gain greatly decreased gravity for a short time. After a short delay, your next skill fired will become CHARGED and deal 200% damage. 

While moving, perform a dash in your current movement direction with 75% reduced cooldown.  

* Whether you're considered moving or not is based exclusively on key input and not velocity.

12 second cooldown.
