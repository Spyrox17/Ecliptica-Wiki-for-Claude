== Shield Mage ==
You are a kind of sorcerer who was always too scared to get hurt. You know what they say, sometimes defense is the best offense. Or something like that.

== Skills ==

=== [[File:Shieldmage-Primary.png|frameless|64x64px]] Primary: Offensive Strike ===
Throw your shield forward, dealing 50 <font COLOR="GREY">PHYSICAL</font> damage. While using Defensive Stance, bash your shield forward, dealing 25 <font COLOR="GREY">PHYSICAL</font> damage and knocking enemies back.

=== [[File:Shieldmage-Secondary.png|frameless|64x64px]] Secondary: Defensive Stance ===
Hold to defend with your shield, blocking incoming damage that hits it and reducing your movement speed by X%.

When blocking attacks, charge up ICEBURST. When lowering your shield, Deal heavy <font COLOR="LIGHTBLUE">FROST</font> damage in an X meter radius based on the number of ICEBURST charges.

=== [[File:Shieldmage-Utility.png|frameless|64x64px]] Utility: Evasive Maneuver ===
For 5 seconds stop yourself in place, creating an impenetrable shield around you and cleansing negative status effects. The shield will reduce <font COLOR="PURPLE">PURPLE</font> attack damage by 50% for yourself and you will gain increased [[Aggro]] draw. While using Defensive Stance, perform a short dash in your movement direction with 75% reduced coolown.

16(4) second cooldown.
