== Strategy ==
The Melgor Johnson battle consists of 2 phases. In Phase 2, his moveset is enhanced with extra damage and more electrical attacks.

At any health in Phase 2, Melgor Johnson may clap his cheeks, emitting a massive purple shockwave.
