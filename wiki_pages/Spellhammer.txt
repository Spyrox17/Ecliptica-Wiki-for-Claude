== Spellhammer ==
As someone with a high magic aptitude who decided to become a wild barbarian, conventions and common sense won't be the only things you're smashing into pieces.

== Skills ==

=== [[File:Spellhammer-Primary.png|frameless|64x64px]] Primary: Pummel ===
Swing your hammer to deal 90 <font COLOR="GREY">PHYSICAL</font> damage and knock enemies back.

Will increase your movement speed by two times during the swing, and is directly scaled with your Movement Speed.

=== Secondary: ===

=== [[File:Spellhammer-Jump-Smash.png|frameless|64x64px]] Jump Smash ===
Jump upwards, then release to slam back down and deal 130 <font COLOR="GREY">PHYSICAL</font> damage and knock enemies back. Landing on top of an enemy will deal X extra damage per X based on your initial height and launch you back up.

OR

=== [[File:Spellhammer-Hammer-Throw.png|frameless|64x64px]] Chuck ===
Hold, then release to throw your hammer. Damage scales on how long you have held the throwing charge, deal X-110 <font COLOR="GREY">PHYSICAL</font> Damage.

=== [[File:Spellhammer-Utility-a.png|frameless|64x64px]] Utility: Disperse ===
Release a wide explosion dealing 130 <font COLOR="GREY">PHYSICAL</font> damage, and jump backwards.

8 second cooldown. The cooldown applies even while in the air, though is much slower.
